A note before you book

"Normal for your age" is not a plan.

If you're in your 40s, 50s or early 60s, you've probably noticed some of this:

  • Your energy fades by mid-afternoon, even when you sleep "enough."
  • Your body holds onto weight no matter how disciplined you are.
  • Your focus, drive and recovery aren't what they were five years ago.
  • You had a physical, were told your labs are "fine," and still don't feel fine.

A standard annual physical checks a handful of markers against ranges built for the general population. It's designed to catch disease, not to help you perform at your best for the next 30 years.

Prime42 takes a different approach. We start with a comprehensive panel covering hormones, metabolic, cardiovascular and inflammatory markers. Then a licensed provider reviews it with you one-on-one. If there's something worth addressing, you get a clear, monitored protocol. If there isn't, we'll tell you that too.

This isn't for everyone. It's for people who want data over guesswork, are willing to follow a plan, and see their health as their most valuable long-term investment. If that's you, take the short assessment below. It shows which membership fits your goals.

Questions

Frequently asked

Who are the providers?

All care is delivered by independent, licensed physicians and nurse practitioners in our affiliated medical group. Every clinical decision, including whether treatment is appropriate, is made by your provider.

Will I definitely get a prescription?

No. Prescriptions are issued only when your provider decides they're medically appropriate based on your labs, history and goals. Some members' best plan is lifestyle, nutrition and monitoring, and we'll tell you if that's you.

Where do I get my labs drawn?

At a partner lab location near you (thousands nationwide), or with an at-home draw where available.

Do you take insurance?

Prime42 is a cash-pay membership. Many members pay with an HSA or FSA card, but eligibility depends on your plan. We can provide receipts for reimbursement.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly memberships can be canceled anytime before your next billing date. Executive annual plans are prorated per our membership terms.

What states do you serve?

We're launching in Texas, starting with Dallas–Fort Worth, and expanding to more states. Availability depends on where our providers are licensed.
